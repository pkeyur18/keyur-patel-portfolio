About

Background

I'm a full-stack developer with 8+ years of experience. Right now I work on FDA-regulated medical device software at Philips — systems used to monitor cardiac patients remotely — where I led the security remediation behind a 510(k) submission and I'm designing the identity architecture (Entra ID + AWS Cognito SSO) that clinicians log into every day. I bring the same standard to every project: documented decisions, tested edge cases, and security considered from the start, not added at the end.

Before Philips, I spent two years at IBM building Java systems for enterprise clients — often as the direct point of contact translating business needs into working software — and four years at Infosys building the Angular front ends and Spring Boot APIs underneath them. I also build my own products end to end outside of work, and I use AI tools like Claude Code daily to move faster without cutting corners.

Process

How an engagement runs

Eight steps, every one of them something you'll actually see happen — not internal process for its own sake.

  1. Discovery

    We talk through the problem, not the tech, until it's clear what "done" means.

  2. Planning

    Scope, timeline, and a fixed or hourly estimate you approve before anything's built.

  3. UI/UX

    You see and approve the design before a line of code is written.

  4. Architecture

    The decisions that are expensive to change later get made deliberately, up front.

  5. Development

    Regular check-ins, working software you can look at along the way — no black box.

  6. Testing

    Real edge cases, not just the happy path — the same discipline as regulated software.

  7. Deployment

    Shipped with a rollback plan, not just a hopeful push to production.

  8. Maintenance

    Available after launch for fixes, updates, and ongoing support.
